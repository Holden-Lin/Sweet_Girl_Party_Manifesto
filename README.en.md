# Sweet Girl Party Manifesto

[简体中文](README.md) | **English**

This repository now separates two layers of writing: the `README` explains the project's core thesis, conceptual framing, and reading path, while the full manifesto lives in dedicated `MANIFESTO` files. The central claim is straightforward: in an era of fragmented attention, weakened institutions, and chronic polarization, `sweetness` is often more transmissible, actionable, and depolarizing than `goodness`.

## Why "Sweetness" Instead of "Goodness"

- `Sweetness` is pre-cognitive; `goodness` is usually post-judgment. Goodness often depends on moral frameworks, reflective capacity, and sustained attention. Sweetness can be felt before it is formally interpreted.
- `Sweetness` spreads through emotional contagion, while `goodness` often relies on institutional mediation. When law, public discourse, education, and shared attention are all deteriorating, sweetness keeps moving through ordinary human contact.
- `Sweetness` is harder to weaponize. Goodness can slide into moral binaries and punitive logic; sweetness, as an affective and relational quality, does not naturally produce a guilty/innocent structure.
- `Sweetness` has a lower barrier to entry. A gentle tone, a glass of water, a patient reply, or a sincere smile can all become repeatable social practices.
- `Sweetness` is better suited to depolarization. It does not begin by sorting people into camps; it begins by restoring emotional connection, which then makes cooperation, justice, and longer-term action more possible.

## Sweetness as an Anti-Entropy Strategy

This project treats fragmented attention, emotional polarization, and social atomization as forms of rising social entropy. Traditional appeals to "goodness" often try to restore order from above through moral or institutional structure, but those structures are themselves under erosion. `Sweetness` works differently: it injects negentropy into local interactions by increasing trust, lowering hostility, and rebuilding connection until order can emerge again in a distributed way.

In that sense, sweetness is not a vague slogan. It is a small-scale, high-frequency social technology.

## Responses to Common Critiques

- `Sweetness` is not a substitute for justice. It rebuilds the social substrate that justice needs in order to function: empathy, trust, and minimum interpersonal warmth.
- `Sweetness` is not covert emotional manipulation. Manipulation is one-way, opaque, and designed for the manipulator's benefit; sweetness is reciprocal, legible, and distributed.
- `Sweetness` is not weakness. It does not deny the need to resist systemic violence; it argues that higher moral action cannot scale on top of total coldness and total polarization.
